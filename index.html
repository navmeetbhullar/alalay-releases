Alalay

A voice assistant for Windows.

You talk to Alalay in your own words, and it does the work on your computer: it can write articles with ChatGPT and save or publish them on your Blogspot blogs from a Google Sheet, and it can test your websites on a schedule.

Alalay is in a private pilot. Installers for pilot users are on the releases page.
