Alalay privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What Alalay is

Alalay is a voice assistant that runs on your own Windows computer. It works in your own Chrome and, if you sign in with Google, with your own Google Sheets and Blogger blogs.

Your Google data

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Alalay asks Google for permission to:

  • see your Google account's email address, so it knows which account a blog belongs to;
  • read and write the Google Sheet you choose, to read your article topics and write each one's status and link;
  • manage your Blogger blogs, to save articles as drafts or publish them on the blog you assigned.

Alalay uses this access only to do the tasks you ask for. Your Google data is handled on your own computer and goes only between your computer and Google. It is not sent to Alalay's makers or to anyone else, it is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Alalay's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your Google sign-in is stored encrypted on your computer. You can remove it at any time in Alalay's Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Images

Images made for your articles are stored on Cloudinary so your blog can show them. They are public images, as on any blog.

Usage data and error reports

To improve Alalay, it sends anonymous usage data and error reports to Firebase (a Google service): what you asked with emails, numbers and passwords removed, what Alalay understood, whether each step worked and how long it took, crashes, and how busy the computer was. It never sends passwords, keys, the contents of your articles, sheets or documents, or screenshots. You are told about this when you set Alalay up, and you can turn it off in Settings.
